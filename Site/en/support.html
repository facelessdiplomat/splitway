Splitway Support

Splitway is a WireGuard client for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Only what you choose goes through the tunnel: chosen apps on a Mac, chosen services on iPhone and iPad.

Contact

Write to SUPPORT_EMAIL. Please include your device, its system version, the Splitway version from Settings and what you did before the problem. If Splitway crashed or a tunnel misbehaves, attach a report from Log › Send Report…: it contains no keys.

Questions

Where do I get a configuration?

From your VPN provider or your own WireGuard server. Splitway does not offer VPN servers: it connects to the ones you add.

How do I add a tunnel?

Import a .conf or .zip file, paste a configuration from the clipboard, scan a QR code on iPhone or iPad, or create a tunnel from scratch.

My Mac asks me to allow a network extension

Splitway sends the traffic of the chosen apps through its network extension. Turn on Splitway in System Settings › General › Login Items & Extensions › Network Extensions. On first launch Splitway guides you through it.

Part of an Apple app's traffic bypasses the tunnel

System services make some network requests on behalf of Apple apps, so part of their traffic may go around the tunnel. Splitway marks such apps in the list.

An app or a command-line tool is missing from the list on a Mac

Splitway lists the apps in the Applications folders. Add any other app or tool with Add Tool… or drag it onto the list.

How do chosen services work on iPhone and iPad?

The domains and subnets of the services you choose go through the tunnel, and everything else goes directly. You can add your own services with their domains and subnets.

The server is not responding

The tunnel is connected, but the server has never answered. Check the configuration: the endpoint, the keys, and whether the server is running.

How do I uninstall Splitway from a Mac?

In Settings, choose Uninstall Splitway…: it removes the tunnels with their keys, the VPN configurations, the app's data and the network extension. Then drag Splitway from the Applications folder to the Trash.

Privacy

Splitway collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
