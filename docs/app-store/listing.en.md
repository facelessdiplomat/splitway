## Description — iOS and iPadOS

1231 / 4000

```text
Splitway is a WireGuard client that lets you decide what goes through the tunnel.

Choose services, and Splitway sends their domains and subnets through the tunnel while everything else keeps using your regular connection. Pick from a catalog of popular messengers, social networks, video, music and AI services, or add your own domains and subnets.

FEATURES
• Selected services: only the services you choose use the tunnel
• All traffic mode, with an option to keep local networks outside the tunnel
• Import .conf and .zip files, paste a configuration, scan a QR code or create a tunnel from scratch
• Edit tunnels and export them as files
• Turn a tunnel on from Control Center, the Lock Screen and the Shortcuts app
• Tunnel details: latest handshake, data received and sent
• A log with export, and crash reports you send yourself
• English and Russian

PRIVACY
Splitway collects no data: no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. Configurations and keys stay on your device, in the keychain, and Splitway never contacts the developer's servers.

YOUR SERVER
Splitway does not provide VPN servers. It connects to WireGuard servers from your VPN provider or your own.

WireGuard is a registered trademark of Jason A. Donenfeld.
```

Splitway is a WireGuard client for Mac that lets you decide which apps use the tunnel.

Choose apps, such as browsers, messengers, developer tools or command-line utilities, and only their traffic goes through the tunnel, DNS queries included. Everything else keeps using your regular connection.

• Selected apps, together with the processes they start, such as commands run in a selected Terminal

PRIVACY
